FAQ

Is this QR code generator really free?

Yes. Generation happens entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server, and there's no limit on how many codes you create.

Do these QR codes expire?

No. Since the QR code encodes your content directly (a "static" code), it never expires and works forever, offline included.

Can I use this for commercial purposes?

Yes, codes generated here are free to use for personal or commercial projects.
