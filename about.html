About QuickQR

QuickQR is a free, browser-based QR code generator. Everything happens on your device — your text, links, and any logo you upload are never sent to a server, so there is nothing to store and nothing to leak.

The goal is simple: a fast, no-signup tool for generating custom QR codes for links, WiFi networks, business cards, menus, and anything else you need to encode.
